# Noteable

Made by KBM for our class project. It's a place to jot down notes, sketch something, and sort it out later.

## Getting started

[**Download Noteable (.zip)**](https://github.com/GitTTAM/Noteable/archive/refs/heads/main.zip)

The ZIP includes all app files and fonts from the latest version on main. You can also use **Code > Download ZIP** on GitHub.

Extract the ZIP, open the Noteable-main folder, then open preview.html in your browser. You can switch between the phone, 14-inch laptop, and 24-inch monitor views. Click Use without frame if you'd rather use the app on its own, or open index.html directly.

Keep the files and fonts folder together. There's nothing to install, and you don't need an account, server, or .env file.

## Taking notes

Type in Quick capture and click Tear off & save. Ctrl + Enter works too. Your note goes into Inbox, and a fresh note is ready. Titles and colors are up to you.

New field adds another section to a note. You could call it Questions, Tasks, or whatever helps. Fields with the same name show up in the same notebook column.

Use Draw to sketch with your mouse, finger, or stylus. Type switches back to writing. Both stay saved, and Undo stroke removes the last line you drew.

## Coming back to your notes

My notebook is where you can search, edit, pin, or move notes into collections. Click a note to open it, then click Save changes when you're done. If you close the editor first, those edits won't be kept.

Delete note asks before removing anything. If you like a note's layout, Save as template lets you reuse its field names and color from the Start from menu.

Add a reminder opens the date and time fields. They start at the current date and time, so change them to when you want the reminder. You'll find pending ones under Reminders, where you can mark them done. Alerts only show while the app is open or when you come back.

## Keeping your work

Drafts save as you go. Your notes stay in this browser, so they won't appear on another device. Clearing browser data deletes them.

Export notes is a placeholder for now. Clicking it doesn't download anything yet.

You can press N to jump to Quick capture when you're not typing. Escape closes a popup or editor.

The font is IBM Plex Mono. Its license is in fonts/OFL.txt.
