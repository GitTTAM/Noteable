Help / FAQ

What's a field?

It's a section of your note. Give it a name like Questions or Tasks. In My notebook, sections with the same name sit in the same column. Pick a suggested name if you want to use one again.

What about collections?

Think of them as folders. New notes land in Inbox. Open a note and change its collection to keep your classes or projects together.

How do I use a template?

Cornell notes is ready when you open the app: questions on the left, notes on the right, and a summary below. Start from also has To-do list and Class notes. Want your own setup? Open a saved note and click Save as template. It keeps the layout, field names, and colors, with none of your writing.

Can I type and draw in the same field?

Yep. Click Draw to sketch, or Type to write. Switching keeps both. Sketches stay as pictures; the app doesn't read your handwriting.

How do reminders work?

Click Add a reminder at the top right of your note, then pick a date and time. It starts at right now, so set it to when you need a nudge. You'll see the reminder here while the app is open or when you come back.

Oops, I deleted a note. Now what?

Click Undo right away, or head to Recently Deleted and hit Restore. Your writing, sketches, collection, and reminder all come back. Deleted notes stay there unless you clear your browser data.

Do I need to sign in?

Nope. Sign in and Sign up are just demo screens for our class project. Use made-up details if you try them. They don't create accounts or send anything.

Will my notes still be here later?

Yes, in this browser on this device. Drafts save as you type. Ctrl + Enter saves a new note. When editing one, click Save changes. Clearing browser data deletes your notes. Export notes doesn't do anything yet.
